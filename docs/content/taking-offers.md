The **Market** tab lists offers posted in the last 3 hours. Filter them by currency, and by whether the person
wants to buy or sell bitcoin.

Each offer shows:

- **the price**: the market price plus or minus a premium. `+1%` means the bitcoin costs 1% more than the market
  price, and `-1%` means 1% less. The app shows where that market price comes from.
- **payment**: the ways the other person can send or receive the money, like PIX, Zelle, Revolut or cash.
- **bitcoin via**: how the bitcoin side is sent, like lightning, onchain or liquid.
- **a description**: limits, hours or conditions, in the maker's own words.

## Before you contact anyone

Open the offer and check **the maker's trade history**. It shows what you said about them in past trades, and what
the people you trust said about them. See [Trading safely](../safety/) for how to read it.

No history isn't a red flag in itself: everyone starts with none. It just means you should trade even less.

## Contact and agree

Tap **Contact maker** to open a private chat. Everything is decided there:

1. the exact amount of money and of bitcoin,
2. the payment details, like the PIX key or the lightning invoice,
3. **who goes first**.

The trade appears under **Trades → Ongoing**. Once it's done, tap **Conclude** to say how it went. That publishes a
short report about the other person, which helps you and the people who trust you next time. **Archive** puts the
trade away without a report.
